# Responsible Use

Generated advertisements are drafts. Automated checks reduce risk but do not guarantee:

- factual accuracy;
- advertising, medical, financial, legal, or regulatory compliance;
- copyright or asset rights;
- platform policy compliance; or
- real-world advertising performance.

Human review is required before publication. Restricted categories require additional review.
Do not fabricate reviews, testimonials, certifications, or health and performance claims.
Voice cloning is not supported. Do not use cloned voices or celebrity likenesses.

Stage 12 Hindi generation is opt-in and has not been reviewed by a native speaker. Hindi
readability is not measured; starter Hindi quality terms require native-speaker review and are not
a certification of translation accuracy or cultural suitability. Hindi PDF/ZIP export remains
blocked until a Devanagari-capable font is integrated.

Brand-kit rights confirmation is user-provided and does not verify that you own or may use a logo
or font. Upload only assets you are authorized to use. Raster logos are re-encoded before local
campaign storage; SVG is rejected. Brand palette contrast warnings do not replace human visual
review.
