# Audience Personas

The personas describe needs and behaviors, not assumptions based on ethnicity, nationality, income, gender, or age alone. A persona may overlap with others. Implementation must use the exact IDs and names in `data/personas.json`.

## college_students
**College Students** | Approx. age 18-24

- Interests: campus life, peer recommendations, value, entertainment.
- Motivations: affordability, convenience, belonging, personal expression.
- Pain points: limited budgets, busy schedules, information overload.
- Objections: perceived high price, unclear usefulness, distrust of polished claims.
- Tone: friendly, energetic, direct.
- Vocabulary: conversational and plain; light slang tolerance only when natural.
- Emotional triggers: self-expression, peer approval, simple rewards.
- Best platforms: Instagram, X, WhatsApp.
- Preferred formats: short video, restrained humorous posts, carousels.
- Avoid: condescension, forced slang, corporate jargon.

## young_professionals
**Young Professionals** | Approx. age 25-34

- Interests: career growth, time management, quality, wellness.
- Motivations: efficiency, professional credibility, convenience, long-term value.
- Pain points: limited time, context switching, decision fatigue.
- Objections: unclear return on effort, premium price, incompatibility with routine.
- Tone: confident, concise, practical.
- Vocabulary: modern professional; slang tolerance minimal.
- Emotional triggers: control, readiness, effortless competence.
- Best platforms: LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp.
- Preferred formats: explainers, before-and-after, short video.
- Avoid: corporate filler, shouting, empty lifestyle claims.

## working_parents
**Working Parents** | Approx. age 30-49

- Interests: family routines, time-saving, safety, durability.
- Motivations: reliability, less mental load, family wellbeing, time saved.
- Pain points: competing responsibilities, unexpected costs, unpredictable schedules.
- Objections: extra maintenance, poor durability, child-safety concerns.
- Tone: warm, reassuring, efficient.
- Vocabulary: clear everyday language; low slang tolerance.
- Emotional triggers: relief, care, preparedness, reliability.
- Best platforms: Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp.
- Preferred formats: demos, quick tips, comparison lists.
- Avoid: parenting judgment, fear-based messaging, long setup instructions.

## fitness_enthusiasts
**Fitness Enthusiasts** | Approx. age 18-45

- Interests: training, outdoor activity, performance habits, recovery.
- Motivations: progress, consistency, product durability, measurable utility.
- Pain points: equipment that fails, time pressure, inconvenient routines.
- Objections: weak performance evidence, uncomfortable use, overhyped marketing.
- Tone: energetic, disciplined, specific.
- Vocabulary: familiar fitness terms explained when needed; moderate slang tolerance.
- Emotional triggers: momentum, achievement, personal bests.
- Best platforms: Instagram, X, Facebook.
- Preferred formats: action video, short demonstrations, routine posts.
- Avoid: body shaming, guaranteed results, extreme challenge language.

## senior_citizens
**Senior Citizens** | Age 60+

- Interests: independence, health maintenance, community, leisure.
- Motivations: comfort, independence, simplicity, trustworthy support.
- Pain points: small text, complex interfaces, unclear return policies.
- Objections: difficulty using the product, lack of support, scam concerns.
- Tone: respectful, calm, clear.
- Vocabulary: plain and accessible; no slang.
- Emotional triggers: independence, confidence, security, connection.
- Best platforms: Facebook, WhatsApp.
- Preferred formats: large-text visuals, step-by-step demos, support information.
- Avoid: age stereotypes, patronizing wording, small-print urgency.

## small_business_owners
**Small Business Owners** | Age varies

- Interests: operations, cash flow, customer retention, productivity.
- Motivations: return on investment, ease of adoption, operational reliability.
- Pain points: uncertain costs, staff training time, limited resources.
- Objections: weak evidence, integration difficulty, vendor lock-in.
- Tone: direct, credible, commercial.
- Vocabulary: business language without unexplained jargon; low slang tolerance.
- Emotional triggers: control, growth, confidence, time saved.
- Best platforms: LinkedIn, Facebook, WhatsApp.
- Preferred formats: case studies, cost-benefit explainers, live demos.
- Avoid: invented ROI claims, startup hype, feature-only descriptions.

## tech_enthusiasts
**Tech Enthusiasts** | Approx. age 18-50

- Interests: new technology, product design, compatibility, testing.
- Motivations: innovation, specification detail, customization, performance.
- Pain points: vague marketing, compatibility issues, limited controls.
- Objections: incomplete specifications, planned obsolescence, weak implementation.
- Tone: precise, curious, informed.
- Vocabulary: technical when useful and defined when needed.
- Emotional triggers: discovery, mastery, smart design.
- Best platforms: X, short-form video channels, LinkedIn.
- Preferred formats: spec breakdowns, hands-on reviews, comparison charts.
- Avoid: buzzword stacking, vague AI claims, unsupported benchmarks.

## luxury_shoppers
**Luxury Shoppers** | Age varies

- Interests: craftsmanship, design, exclusivity, service.
- Motivations: quality perception, lasting value, distinctive design, premium experience.
- Pain points: mass-market styling, inconsistent service, uncertain provenance.
- Objections: price without evident quality, poor materials, generic brand story.
- Tone: polished, restrained, sensory.
- Vocabulary: elevated but readable; no slang.
- Emotional triggers: taste, craft, rarity, status.
- Best platforms: Instagram, Facebook.
- Preferred formats: editorial photography, craft stories, short brand films.
- Avoid: hard-sell pressure, logos without substance, appearance-based exclusion.

## budget_conscious_shoppers
**Budget-Conscious Shoppers** | Age varies

- Interests: price comparison, durability, discounts, practical use.
- Motivations: low total cost, dependability, clear value, avoiding wasted spending.
- Pain points: hidden fees, low-quality substitutes, complex offers.
- Objections: price, warranty limitations, unclear cost advantage.
- Tone: honest, straightforward, helpful.
- Vocabulary: plain and specific; low slang tolerance.
- Emotional triggers: smart savings, confidence, financial control.
- Best platforms: Facebook, WhatsApp, X.
- Preferred formats: price comparisons, deal alerts, durability demonstrations.
- Avoid: false scarcity, hidden terms, shaming low budgets.

## rural_first_time_online_buyers
**Rural / First-Time Online Buyers** | Age varies

- Interests: practical utility, delivery clarity, community trust, local relevance.
- Motivations: trust, simple purchasing, available support, real-world usefulness.
- Pain points: unclear delivery, limited access, payment or setup uncertainty.
- Objections: online fraud concern, returns difficulty, mobile-data concerns.
- Tone: patient, grounded, trustworthy.
- Vocabulary: simple, region-neutral; low slang tolerance.
- Emotional triggers: trust, practical independence, support availability.
- Best platforms: WhatsApp, Facebook.
- Preferred formats: step-by-step visuals, demonstrations, support previews.
- Avoid: urban assumptions, jargon, dark patterns.
