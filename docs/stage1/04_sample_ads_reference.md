## What Makes a Good Ad

- Uses only features and claims that the user supplied or that are verified and documented.
- Names a specific audience need instead of relying on broad demographics.
- Leads with one clear benefit and supports it with one relevant detail.
- Matches the platform's length, format, and reading behavior.
- Uses a CTA that names the next action, not vague pressure.
- Sounds consistent with the campaign brief and brand voice.
- Includes a human-review cue for safety-sensitive claims or high-impact decisions.
- Avoids fake scarcity, copied slogans, unsupported statistics, stereotypes, and dark patterns.
- Gives the reader enough context to understand the product without a separate package or wall of text.
- Offers a genuine audience variation rather than simply changing adjectives.
